# **RAVE RAID** — patch notes

*26 August 2026 · beta*

**The biggest single day the floor has had.** New records, glowsticks you can
actually pour, a light rig that knows where the floor is, and the safety
controls follow you onto the ring.

## 🎧 The record box

- **Two new raid records.** DEFENSE (126 BPM) and AWAKENING (165 BPM). The box
  is now 26 records, 22 of them charted for raids
- **Two records took their proper names.** V ONE is now **ORIGINAL** and VFALL
  is now **BREAKCORE V4**. Your scores and your cued song carry over untouched
- **Both new records land on the drop.** DEFENSE opens on twenty-two seconds of
  stripped intro and AWAKENING spends half a minute arriving, so the needle
  goes down where the track actually starts

## ✨ Your glowsticks

- **They are full of liquid now.** Frosted tubes with a real pour inside, and
  the surface stays level however you tilt, swing or roll them
- **Dancing sloshes them end to end.** The glow surges with your hands, and a
  rewarded swap churns the pour along with the flash and the kick
- **Thicker in the hand.** 32 mm batons instead of 26 mm, so the liquid has
  body from arm's length
- **Sparks fly where you throw them.** A hard sideways fling now sends the whole
  burst sideways instead of fountaining straight up with a lean
- **Fixed: turn a stick on its side and you could see straight through the
  liquid.** The surface can no longer leave the glass
- **Both ends are the same now.** No grip cap, no handedness. The paying end
  announces itself by paying

## 🪩 The show

- **Your platform rim is a real neon tube.** It used to be a see-through
  outline; now it is a solid lit fixture with the glow bleeding around it
- **Platforms light the void they float in.** A pool of your seat colour hangs
  under every deck
- **The stage floor turns.** A chasing LED tick ring one way, a ring of eight
  dashes the other, hues marching with the bars, and light pooling on the floor
  around the stage
- **Lasers stop passing through platforms.** Every beam and mirror-ball shaft
  now lands where it lands, leaving a hot spot or a pool of light on the deck
  it hits
- **Beams fade out instead of ending in mid-air.** Light through haze, not
  glowing sticks
- **The club's stage footlights stopped twisting.** Seven full rounds along the
  lip, reading as lamps from every seat instead of crescents pointing different
  ways

## 🕺 The dance

- **The groove pays when you dodge.** The judge used to read straight up and
  down, so a swap thrown mid-lunge stopped counting exactly when holding the
  rhythm was hardest. It now reads the throw between your hands, so a groove
  carried sideways through a dodge keeps paying
- **Every pose that paid before still pays.** A level T-pose and a close-in
  wobble still stay silent
- **The expert duck donut serves its tight middle everywhere.** The rarest move
  in the game was quietly handing you a roomier middle than the plain donut
  beside it

## 🛡️ Safety

- **MUTE and BLOCK follow you onto the ring.** The safety console now has a
  home on the mid-set pause card as well as the club floor, so a dancer who
  turns unpleasant halfway through a record is no longer someone you have to
  finish the record with
- **Your mic and the voice switch ride along with it**

## 📋 The board

- **The podium header is gone.** It sat directly under the mirror ball and
  spent the whole podium half-eclipsed. Rank 1 in gold wearing the night's
  letter was always the real headline
